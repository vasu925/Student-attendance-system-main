Conditions for using the attendance system.

Authorized use

Use this system only if authorized by the educational institution or system administrator. Keep credentials confidential and sign out when using a shared device.

Users entering student details and attendance must ensure entries are accurate, timely, and supported by institutional procedures. Report errors to the administrator promptly.

Do not attempt to access another person's account, bypass permissions, interfere with the service, upload malicious content, or disclose student information without authorization.
