Privacy Policy

How this attendance system handles information.

Effective date: 9 November 2026

This Student Attendance Management System is intended for authorized educational use. It may process faculty account details, student names, roll numbers, department and year information, contact details supplied by users, and attendance records.

Use of information

Information is used to authenticate users, maintain student and subject records, record attendance, and generate reports. It should not be used for unrelated purposes.

Access and disclosure

Access should be limited to authorized users according to their assigned role. Do not share credentials or export files with unauthorized people. Information may be disclosed where required by applicable law.

Storage and security

Records are stored in the configured database. The operator should protect credentials, restrict database access, use HTTPS, maintain backups, and apply suitable retention practices. No internet-connected system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Retention and requests

Records should be retained only as long as required for legitimate academic and legal purposes. Contact the institution or system administrator to request correction or deletion, subject to applicable requirements.
